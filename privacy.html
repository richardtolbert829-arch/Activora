Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Activora TV (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses and safeguards information when you visit our website or contact us. By using this website, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, device type and message when you submit our contact form or email us.
  • Automatically collected information: basic technical data such as browser type, device type, pages visited and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies: small files used to keep the site working and, where you consent, to understand how visitors use it.

We do not ask for or collect streaming account passwords, payment card numbers, bank details or one-time security codes.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To operate, maintain and improve our website and guides.
  • To monitor for abuse, fraud and security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers (such as website hosting, email or analytics providers) who process it on our behalf under confidentiality obligations, or where required by law.

4. Cookies and analytics

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the website.

5. Data retention

We keep contact messages only as long as needed to respond to you and for reasonable record-keeping, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

6. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to processing. To make a request, contact us at support@yourdomain.com.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Third-party links

Our guides may link to official websites of streaming providers and device makers. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

11. Contact us
